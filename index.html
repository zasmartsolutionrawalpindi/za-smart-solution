Brand Protection

Trademark Registration Services

Protect your brand name and logo. We can guide you through the general trademark application steps in Pakistan.

Registration depends on official examination and applicable legal requirements. No approval or timeline is guaranteed.

Visit IPO-Pakistan ↗

Trademark Registration Process (Pakistan)

1

Brand Search & Consultation

Check for similar names or logos and identify relevant trademark class(es).

2

Prepare the Application

Gather applicant details, brand representation and goods/services information.

3

File with IPO-Pakistan

Submit the appropriate application and pay the applicable official fee.

4

Examination & Publication

The application is examined; objections or opposition may need a response.

5

Registration Decision

If all requirements are met, the mark may proceed to registration.

Why Register a Trademark?

  • Helps protect your brand identity
  • Can support action against confusingly similar use
  • Builds recognition and customer trust
  • Supports long-term brand development

Fees, forms, classes and current filing procedures should be confirmed directly with IPO-Pakistan or a qualified professional.
